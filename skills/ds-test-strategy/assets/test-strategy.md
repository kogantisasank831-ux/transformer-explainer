## Isolation, resources and maintenance

Document deterministic seams, secrets/data handling, real integration versus mocks, cleanup, hardware/service needs, flaky-test treatment and relevant deep-evaluation measurement contracts.

## Status and decisions

Separate proposed, implemented and actually executed checks; record scoped blockers, target acceptance sources, CI/server enforcement gaps and links to project logic. Never invent pass results, coverage thresholds or runnable commands for missing suites.
