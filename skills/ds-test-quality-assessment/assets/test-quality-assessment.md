# Test quality assessment

- Scope / source-test-logic revisions / evidence inspected:

| Rule or risk | Existing test/oracle and actual boundary | Supported gap / confidence | Evidence / prioritized remedy |
|---|---|---|---|

## Findings and verification

Give concrete locations, trigger or counterexample, impact/severity and focused next tasks. Record commands, collection/results, real versus reported evidence, any bounded mutation scope and missing coverage without inventing scores.

## Limits

Separate observed test weakness from unproven product failure, policy questions, unavailable environments and unverified CI/server/model/security evidence. Preserve review-only scope.
