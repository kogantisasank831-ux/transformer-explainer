# UX and product acceptance record

- Product/candidate, rule IDs, decision, owners and authorized business acceptors:
- Intended/excluded users, context, end-to-end journey, research questions, sample and consent/data boundary:

| Need / criterion | Scenario and expected user/business outcome | Research / UAT evidence | Defect or uncertainty | Decision / owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Candidate and evidence

Record environment/config/schema/integration/data/model identities, participant/task limits, completion/correctness and qualitative findings, backend state, external effects, cleanup, failed or blocked cases and retests.

## Acceptance and handoff

List accepted, rejected, conditional and not-tested scope. For conditions record risk owner, expiry and closure evidence. Preserve open process questions, residual risks, design/logic updates and next research or acceptance action.
