# Accessibility verification record

- Product/version, rule IDs, owners, accepted standard/level, legal or contractual source and claim boundary:
- Pages/components/states/languages/roles, viewports/devices, third parties, AT/browser combinations, environment and test data:

| Journey / component / state | Criterion or rule | Automated / manual / AT evidence | User impact and finding | Owner / retest |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Coverage and findings

Record semantic, keyboard/focus, forms/errors, visual/reflow, motion/media, dynamic update, chart/generated-content and assistive-technology results. List reviewed false positives, suppressions, inaccessible scope and exceptions.

## Claim and handoff

State exact fixes and regression tests, retest results, unresolved barriers, third-party dependencies, user-testing boundary, residual risk, owner approval and next review. Do not state conformance beyond the tested and accepted scope.
