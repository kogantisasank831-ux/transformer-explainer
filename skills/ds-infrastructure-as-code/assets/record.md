## State, security, and drift

Record locking, backup/recovery, secrets, identity permissions, imports/moves, intentional exceptions, network/data boundaries, quotas/cost, policy results, and drift classification.

## Execution status

List validate/test/plan commands and checksums, plan review and approval status, apply/destroy/import/state-operation status, post-apply evidence if any, failures, residual risks, owners, and next actions.
