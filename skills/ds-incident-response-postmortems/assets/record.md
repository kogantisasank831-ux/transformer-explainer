## Stabilization and recovery

Record containment, mitigation, evidence preservation, user and data/model checks, backlog or duplicate reconciliation, recovery criteria, stability window, remaining risks and closure authority.

## Postmortem and actions

Record impact, detection, trigger, contributing conditions, safeguards, response and communication learning. List each corrective action with outcome, type, owner, due date, dependencies, status and independent closure evidence. State unresolved uncertainty and accepted risk.
