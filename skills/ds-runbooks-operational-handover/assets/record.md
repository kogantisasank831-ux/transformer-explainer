# Runbook and operational handover record

- Procedure or service, rule IDs, accountable owners, operators, authority and environments:
- Trigger, prerequisites, versions, access path, dependencies, prohibited conditions and expected duration:

| Step / branch | Exact target and action | Expected evidence | Stop / recovery / cleanup | Exercise result |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Freshness and validation

Record author/reviewer, last review, last successful cold-read or drill, compatible versions, untestable steps, failures, broken links, change triggers and next review.

## Handover readiness

List inventory coverage, receiving people, access, training, paired and reverse-shadow scenarios, on-call/escalation readiness, known risks and open actions. Record giving and receiving owner acceptance separately and state any blocker to safe ownership.
