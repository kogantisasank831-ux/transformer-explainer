# Cost and resource optimization checks

- Define scope, period, currency, pricing, taxes/support, credits/discounts, amortization and allocation; reconcile to the billing or ledger source and expose shared/unallocated cost.
- Tie cost to accountable workload and demand. State unit-cost numerator/denominator and separate price, usage, volume/mix, allocation and one-time effects.
- Use representative utilization distributions and peaks across compute, storage, database/query, network, observability, licenses and provider APIs; average CPU is insufficient.
- Preserve redundancy, headroom, quotas, startup, recovery and quality. Evaluate commitments or interruptible capacity only against stable or tolerant demand.
- For DS/ML, include experiment lifetime, preprocessing/training, GPU use, artifacts, feature/index storage, tokens, inference/provider use and quality-safe model alternatives.
- Give each option gross/net estimate, implementation cost, confidence, dependencies, interaction, reversibility and performance/reliability/security/privacy/quality guardrails.
- Preview exact targets and use versioned authorized changes; protect required data and recovery. Recommendations, budgets and forecasts do not authorize deletion or purchase.
- Verify realized savings after billing lag while normalizing demand and checking guardrails, shifted cost and toil; keep forecast and realized savings distinct.

Use the FinOps Foundation's current [Usage Optimization capability](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/usage-optimization/) and [FinOps capabilities](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/) as primary process references while retaining product-specific quality and authority boundaries.
